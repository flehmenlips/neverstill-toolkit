Neverstill CFO has been disconnected

Your QuickBooks Online company is no longer connected to Neverstill CFO.

What this means

  • The app can no longer read any data from this QuickBooks Online company.
  • The connection keys Intuit issued to the app no longer work.
  • Nothing in your QuickBooks data was changed. The app is read-only.
  • Reports that were already saved on the owner’s private systems stay there until the owner deletes them.

Did you mean to disconnect?

If this was a mistake, you can reconnect at any time. Go to the Neverstill CFO page and follow the connect steps. The owner starts the connection from his private system, and you approve it on Intuit’s secure sign-in page. Each company is connected separately.

How to disconnect another company

  1. Sign in to QuickBooks Online as an admin of the company.
  2. Open Apps (in some versions: Settings ⚙ > Apps or Manage apps) and go to My apps.
  3. Find Neverstill CFO and choose Disconnect.
