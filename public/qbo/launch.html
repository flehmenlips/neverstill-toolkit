Neverstill CFO

A private, read-only QuickBooks Online reporting tool

Neverstill CFO helps George Page keep an eye on the books of his own companies. It reads accounting data from QuickBooks Online and turns it into financial reports and answers to everyday money questions, such as cash on hand, profit and loss, what is owed, and what is due.

This is a private, internal tool. It is not available to the public and is not listed on the QuickBooks App Store. It connects only to these QuickBooks Online companies:
  • Neverstill Developers LLC
  • Neverstill Ranch LLC
  • Coq au Vin LLC
  • George & Rose (personal)

What it can and cannot do

  • Can: read accounts, transactions, customers, vendors, invoices, bills, and reports.
  • Cannot: create, change, or delete anything in QuickBooks. Those functions are turned off.
  • Data stays on the owner’s private systems. It is never sold or used for advertising.

Connect or reconnect a company

Connecting always goes through Intuit’s own secure sign-in. The owner starts the connection from his private system. You are then sent to Intuit, where you sign in, pick one of the companies above, and approve read access. Intuit then sends you back and the connection is saved.

To reconnect a company that was disconnected, or whose connection expired, the owner runs the same connection step again. Each company is connected separately.

Disconnect

You can remove the app’s access at any time in QuickBooks Online: open Apps > My apps, find Neverstill CFO, and choose Disconnect. See Disconnect help.
