Privacy Policy

Neverstill CFO · Effective date: October 6, 2026

This policy explains what information the Neverstill CFO app accesses, how it is used, and how access can be removed. It is written in plain English on purpose.

1. Who we are

Neverstill CFO is made and run by Neverstill Developers LLC, 10640 Freeman Road, Birkenfeld, OR 97016 (“we” or “us”). Questions go to george@neverstill.dev.

2. What this app is

Neverstill CFO is a private, internal tool. It is not offered to the public and is not listed on the QuickBooks App Store. Only our owner, George Page, uses it, and only with QuickBooks Online companies that he owns or controls:

  • Neverstill Developers LLC
  • Neverstill Ranch LLC
  • Coq au Vin LLC
  • George & Rose (personal)

We do not accept connections from other people or businesses.

3. What information the app accesses

When one of the companies above is connected, the app uses Intuit’s QuickBooks Online Accounting API to read that company’s accounting data. This can include the chart of accounts, transactions, invoices, bills, payments, customer and vendor records, and financial reports such as profit and loss, balance sheet, and cash flow.

The app also keeps the connection details that Intuit issues during sign-in (access and refresh tokens and the QuickBooks company ID) so the connection keeps working.

4. Read-only use

The app is used for reading and reporting only. Intuit’s accounting permission could allow changes to data, but our software is set up with its create, update, and delete functions turned off. The app does not add, change, or delete anything in QuickBooks.

5. How the information is used

We use the information only to produce financial reports, bookkeeping reviews, tax preparation support, and business planning for the owner’s own companies. This includes AI assistant software that the owner runs on his own private systems to answer his questions about his own books.

6. Where the information is kept

Information from QuickBooks is stored only on private systems that the owner controls, such as a private Linux server run for the owner and the owner’s own computers. It is not put on public websites. This website (neverstill.dev) does not receive or store any QuickBooks data.

7. No selling or sharing

We do not sell QuickBooks data, rent it, use it for advertising, or share it with other businesses for their own use. The only times data leaves the owner’s systems are:

  • when a service provider processes it on the owner’s behalf to run his tools (for example, the AI assistant service he uses to analyze his own reports), under that provider’s terms;
  • when the owner chooses to send his own reports to his own advisers, such as an accountant, bookkeeper, or attorney; or
  • when the law requires it.

8. How long information is kept

We keep information as long as the owner needs it for his business and tax records. The owner can delete it from his systems at any time.

9. Removing access at any time

Access can be revoked at any time from QuickBooks Online:

  1. Sign in to QuickBooks Online as an admin of the company.
  2. Open Apps (in some versions: Settings ⚙ > Apps or Manage apps) and go to My apps.
  3. Find Neverstill CFO and choose Disconnect.

After a disconnect, the app can no longer read data from that company. Reports already saved on the owner’s systems stay there until the owner deletes them. See Disconnect help for more.

10. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect this information. Access to the systems that hold it is limited to the owner and the tools he runs, and app credentials are kept out of public code and public websites. No system is perfectly secure, and we do not claim any particular certification or audit.

11. Children

This app is not meant for children and does not knowingly collect information about children.

12. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the new version on this page and update the effective date above.
